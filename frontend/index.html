What makes learners finish?

EduMetrics follows 2,500 online courses and 33.3 million learners from 2010 to 2025, finds what keeps people to the end, and trains a model that calls retention before launch.

Every dot is a course

Each glowing point is one of the 2,500 courses, placed by its length and its rating and raised by how much of it learners complete. The landscape beneath is the smoothed average: ridges are where people finish, and the average is 69.3%.

Length and quality decide who finishes

From the peak to the valley. Short, top-rated courses reach 83.6% completion; long, poorly rated ones sink to 50.6%. Every step down in length or rating costs about 8 points.

03 / 05   levels

Advanced courses lose half their learners

Each ring is a course level, filled as far as its average learner gets. Beginners reach 78.9%. Advanced learners stop at 50.4%, the steepest dropout in the data.

ring length = average completion per course level

A model that calls retention early

A logistic regression trained on 1,875 courses sorted the other 625 into these four groups. It is right 81.9% of the time, with 71.9% precision, and finds 56% of the truly high-retention courses.

05 / 05   subjects

Subject barely matters

Eight towers, one per category, rise to their completion rate. All eight sit within 3.5 points: Photography leads at 71.1%, Design trails at 67.7%. Format beats topic.

tower height = average completion, scaled from 60% to 72%

What pushes the prediction

Model coefficients on normalised features. Time actually spent watching lifts the odds of high retention; sheer content length drags them down hardest.

From raw rows to a live dashboard

Four Python scripts run in order, then a routed dashboard in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript reads their exported JSON and embedded Plotly charts.

  1. generate_dataset.py

    Generate

    Builds 2,500 realistic courses from 2010 to 2025, modelled on major platforms.

  2. preprocessing.py

    Clean

    Normalises every feature, scores engagement and labels retention.

  3. analysis.py

    Chart

    Six Seaborn charts and four Plotly interactives, from sunburst to heatmap.

  4. insights.py

    Learn

    Writes the findings as text and trains the retention model.

  5. frontend/

    Show

    A routed dashboard with filters and toasts, plus a generated Word report.
